Our Story

Indianapolis's Emergency Locksmith Since 2011

Indy Locksmith 247 was built for one reason: when Indianapolis residents get locked out at 2am, they deserve a locksmith who shows up in 20 minutes and charges a fair, upfront price — not $400 billed after the job is done.

We're locally owned and operated, serving all of Marion County and the Indianapolis metro area. Every call is answered by a live person. Every job is quoted before we start. Every technician is licensed, background-checked, and insured.
