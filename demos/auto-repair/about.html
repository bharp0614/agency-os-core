Our Story

Honest Repairs for Indianapolis Drivers

Circle City Auto Repair was built on a simple idea: Indianapolis drivers deserve a shop they can trust. No upselling. No unnecessary repairs. Just an honest diagnosis and quality work backed by a real warranty.

We're locally owned and operated out of our shop on E Washington St, serving Marion County and the surrounding Indianapolis area. Every vehicle that comes through our door gets the same care we'd give our own.

Our team is led by James Whitaker, ASE Master Automotive Technician with over 12 years of hands-on experience on domestic and import drivetrains. We work on all makes and models.
