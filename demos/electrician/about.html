Our Story

Indianapolis's Electrician for the Long Haul

Indy Electric Pro was built on a simple belief: homeowners deserve an electrician who answers the phone, pulls the permit, and stands behind the work — whether it's an emergency at midnight or a panel upgrade scheduled two weeks out.

We're locally owned and licensed in Indiana. Every technician is a licensed journeyman or master electrician — not an apprentice sent alone on a job they're not ready for. We serve all of Marion County and the surrounding Indianapolis area.

We Pull the Permit. Every Time.

Unpermitted electrical work is dangerous and can void your homeowner's insurance, complicate a home sale, and leave you liable for fires. We pull the permit on every job that requires one — no exceptions. It's not optional, and any electrician who skips it is cutting corners you can't afford.
